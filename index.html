About us

BITS is a student-run organization at the University of Washington dedicated to bridging the gap between business and technology. We equip students with the technical, analytical, and professional skills needed to succeed in technology-driven industries.

You don't need a technical background to join. Some of our members write SQL every day; others are opening Excel for the first time. Both belong here.

  • Community

    Members come from business, information systems, computer science, and everything adjacent — whether you're just exploring tech or already deep in it.

  • Skills

    Practical work with Excel, SQL, Python, data analysis, product thinking, and technical interviewing, plus the leadership and communication skills that make them count.

  • Network

    Meet students, alumni, and industry guests at every meeting. Past guests have come from Amazon, Meta, and Microsoft, along with interns at JPMorgan and Google.
